## 3 Study design
- Type: questionnaire survey, RII ranking; plus sector-specific RII (Table 4), Kruskal-Wallis, Spearman correlation, and PCA/Varimax factor analysis.
- Country: India. Scope: roads and bridges (p.2); respondents' project experience included buildings 31.3% (p.3).
- Preliminary survey: 50 road construction projects (mainly Public Works Department), delays 1 to 12 months (pp.2-3).
- Questionnaire: factors from literature (Table 1, 25 factors, p.2). Likert scale 1-5 (p.3); p.5 states 1 = very low to 5 = very high. n = 72 construction management professionals (pp.1, 3, 5).
- Respondent mix (p.3): clients 41.33%, consultants 38.90%, contractors 12.50%. Project types: Buildings 31.3%, Roads 33.6%, Underpass/Bridges 21.40%, Utilities 8.41%, Others 5.33%. 77.80% reported delays on ongoing projects. Of delayed projects: 56% delays of 10 to 30%, 25% delays of 31 to 50%, 19% delays of 51 to 100%.
- Method: RII = sum W / (A x N), A = 5 (p.3). Worked example given: RII = 200/360 = 0.55 for "planning and scheduling deficiency" (p.3); note Table 2 prints 0.525 for this factor.
- Factor analysis (p.6-7): PCA with Varimax, Kaiser criterion, loading threshold 0.5, KMO 0.78, Bartlett p<0.001, VIF<5; four factors, 68.3% variance.

## 5 Relevance to brick-masonry risk identification in India
- Indian questionnaire RII study with a transparent item table, so it is usable as a comparison source for generic delay factors: material delivery delay, material shortage, unreliable suppliers, rework, subcontractor delays, weather, site restrictions.
- It is about roads and bridges (infrastructure), not buildings or masonry. No brickwork, labour productivity or labour-shortage item appears in Table 1 or 2. Overlap with the tool's risks is limited to materials and weather (plus generic rework/subcontractor items). Treat as supporting/validation context only.
- Its group-level and item-level RII values are on different bases; do not rank groups against items.

## 6 Limitations
- Internal inconsistencies: the construction site group is 0.555 in Table 3 but "0.602" in the text; owner/client 0.472 (Table 3) vs 0.474 (text); worked example RII 0.55 vs Table 2 0.525 for planning and scheduling; the item "restriction at job site" is 0.602 (rank 4).
- Table 2 omits the External and Project groups, and the item at rank 3 (weather 0.616 appears only in text); the L V M H W column header note labels are inconsistent (V "Very Low", W "Very high").
- Respondent mix is mostly clients and consultants (about 80%); only 12.50% contractors; sampling method and response rate not stated (NR). Likert reliability (e.g. Cronbach alpha) not reported (NR).
- Factor analysis uses 25 variables on n = 72 (a low ratio) and its 25 factors are not the same as the 50 items of Table 2.
- Sector-specific Table 4 gives n per sector as NR; Table 4 values are not in Table 2.
- References contain obvious bibliographic errors (e.g. refs 2, 5); reference 8 etc. not checked.
