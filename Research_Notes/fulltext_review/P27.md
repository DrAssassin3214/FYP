## 3 Study design
- Type: questionnaire survey + RII ranking; then comparison with four machine-learning classifiers (Deep Learning, Neural Network, Decision Tree, Naive Bayes). (p1, p22-23)
- Country/region: Thailand, northeastern region; public-sector construction. (p1, p3)
- Sample: 30 delayed public construction projects (purposive sampling), 380 respondents (state procurement officials, inspection committee members, agency project supervisors, contractors, project management consultants), divided equally among four groups (p13, p14). Population stated 38,000 individuals; 3 survey rounds 150 + 160 + 70; "100% response rate" (p14).
- Scale: 1-5 importance (5 highest ... 1 lowest); RII = sum(W)/(A x N), A = 5, N = 380 (p13-14).
- Factors: 28 delay causes in four groups (contractor 10, employer 8, project manager 5, external 5), derived from literature (Table 1, p6). Cronbach alpha overall 0.849 (p15).
- RII bands (Table 5, p16): 0.0-0.2 very low; 0.2-0.4 low; 0.4-0.6 average; 0.6-0.8 high; 0.8-1.0 very high.
- Not a masonry study; general public construction.

## 5 Relevance to brick-masonry risk identification in India
- General public-sector construction delay study in Thailand, not masonry-specific and not India. Only supporting/validation evidence for generic risk categories.
- Relevant generic items: contractor financial problems, labour shortage (Table 1 p6 describes seasonal shortage, harvesting and festival seasons, which is plausible for Indian site labour but the paper does not study India), delayed materials/equipment availability, subcontractor problems, inefficient planning, inexperienced contractor/subcontractor, inflation of material prices, weather.
- Ranks come from a 380-respondent survey of delayed public projects only, so they are a ranking among delayed projects, not general frequency.
- Cannot be used to claim anything about brick-masonry productivity.

## 6 Limitations
- Only delayed projects sampled; purposive sampling; Thailand public sector only.
- Weighted values (W) are not derivable from RII (W 84.44% vs RII 0.777); method of W not explained.
- Many internal inconsistencies: abstract 0.773 vs 0.733; Cronbach alpha swap between text and Table 4; Table 6 vs Tables 7/8 W values differ (materials 78.89% vs 80.00%; design 80.00% vs 78.89%; pre-construction inspection 77.88% vs 77.78%; late handover 70.56% vs 70.00%; political 71.11% vs 71.67%); Table 8 repeats a row; text refers to Table 8 for project manager group (should be Table 9); "Table 4" cited for 28 item RII; 28 rows but ranks end at 24; 100% response over 3 rounds from a population of 38,000 sampled to 380 not explained.
- RII compared to ML "accuracy" is not a meaningful like-for-like comparison; ML section has text that appears copied from unrelated fields (p20, p8 references to stock options; reference to rTMS depression study).
- Questionnaire not reproduced.
