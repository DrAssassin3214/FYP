## 3 Study design
- Type: questionnaire survey, ranking by Relative Importance Index (RII); mean, SD also reported (p.8).
- Country: Sri Lanka. All types of construction project (road, residential building, commercial building, infrastructure, water/drainage/irrigation, other); not masonry-specific (p.7, p.9 Table 1).
- 39 delay factors from literature review and 10 experts (4 authors of past studies, 6 Sri Lankan civil engineers) (p.7); five groups: client/owner 8, contractor 8, consultant 8, resource 8, external 7 (p.7-8).
- Scale: five-point Likert, 1 (Very low significance) to 5 (Very high significance) (p.8).
- RII = sum(w*x)/(A*N), A=5, N=respondents (Eq 1, p.8).
- Population: qualified civil engineers listed on CIDA website, 2,716; questionnaire sent to 1,416 chosen by random sampling; pilot of 10 engineers; 163 responses (p.8). Respondent types: client/owner 28.8%, contractor 28.2%, consultant 39.9%, other 3.1% (p.8-9).
- Cronbach's alpha: pilot 0.9157, main 0.9505 (p.8). 38 of 163 answered an open-ended question (word cloud, p.16).
- Table 1 demographics (p.9): male 82.8, female 17.2; age 20-29 28.2, 30-39 12.9, 40+ 58.9; Bachelor 42.3, Master 55.2, PhD 1.2, Diploma 0.6, Other 0.6; experience <5y 27, 5-9y 1.8, 10-19y 24.5, 20+ 46.6; organisation: road 26.4, residential 11, commercial 17.2, infrastructure 28.8, water/drainage/irrigation 6.7, other 9.8.

## 5 Relevance to brick-masonry risk identification in India
- Not India and not masonry; a whole-project delay-factor ranking from engineers of many project types. Use only as a general, cross-country support that labour shortage, material delivery delay, material price fluctuation and bad weather appear as delay factors (labour shortage RII 0.8245 rank 2; delivery delay 0.8098 rank 4; price fluctuation 0.7890 rank 6; low labour productivity 0.7485 rank 11; bad weather 0.6528 rank 30, Table 2).
- Low productivity of labourers (Q28) is the only productivity-labelled item; it is about labour generally, not brick laying output.
- Paper reports Covid-19 as a top-ranked factor (rank 5) and says its results are shaped by the pandemic period (pp.15, 19); this limits transfer.
- It does not give masonry-specific factors (mortar, bricks wastage, scaffolding, curing etc.).

## 6 Limitations
- Authors: sample may not be representative of the large population of civil engineers (p.19). 163 of 1,416 sent (about 11.5%; computed here, not printed).
- Respondents are civil engineers only (no site supervisors or masons); 55.2% hold Master's degrees.
- Data gathered during Covid-19; period of data collection (dates) not stated in the text read.
- Likert-based RII reflects perceived significance, not measured delay days.
- Factor list is fixed in advance (39), so unlisted risks are not captured.
