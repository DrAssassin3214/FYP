## 3 Study design
- Type: systematic review (PRISMA) plus meta-analysis ("metadata analysis") of published RII values; secondary data, no new survey.
- Region: Middle East (authors' list includes Bahrain, Cyprus, Egypt, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Palestine, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Turkiye, UAE, Yemen, p. 194). Note Table 4 includes Malaysia and Vietnam as "Middle East" studies (see Limitations).
- Search: Scopus only, titles with "Factors Affecting Construction Productivity" / "...Construction Labour Productivity" / "Factors Influencing Construction Productivity"; 2000-2020 (p. 201). Scopus yielded 73 articles; 65 specifically investigated factors affecting CLP (p. 202). Table 3 (p. 203) lists countries: Iran 4, Egypt 3, Vietnam 3, Saudi Arabia 2, Bahrain/Iraq/Jordan/Kuwait/Malaysia/Oman/Palestine/Qatar/Yemen 1 each, Total 21 (the 65 vs 21 difference is not explained in the text).
- Selection of 10 studies: Middle East, 2000-2020, one article per country, RII values available (p. 203). Excluded for lack of RII: Jalal and Shoar 2019, Ghoddousi and Hosseini 2012, Rad and Kim 2018 (Iran), Al-Rubaye and Mahjoob 2020 (Iraq), Almathami et al. 2017 (Saudi), Hai and Van Tam 2019 (Vietnam) (p. 205).
- Factors: only those in at least 3 of the 10 studies kept: 35 factors (p. 203). Grouped into management, technological, human/worker, external (p. 204).
- RII (Eq. 1, Dey et al. 2017): RII = sum(w) / (A x N) (p. 205).
- Model: random-effects; effect size = RII, sample size = N of each study; Neyeloff, Fuchs and Moreira (2012) Excel spreadsheet; effect summary with 95% CI (pp. 204-205).
- Respondents: N per source study (Table 6, p. 206): Abdul Kadir 2005 N=100; Enshassi 2007 N=105; Jarkas and Bitar 2012 N=180; Jarkas, Kadri and Younes 2012 N=350; Mahamid 2013 N=59; Hafez 2014 N=55; Jarkas 2015 N=59; Jarkas, Al Balushi and Raveendranath 2015 N=132; Alaghbari et al. 2019 N=91; Toan et al. 2020 N=56. (Sum 1187, my arithmetic, not printed.) Respondent type of the source studies: NR in this paper.
- Scale of source studies: NR (RII only; Likert points not stated).

## 5 Relevance to brick-masonry risk identification in India
- It is a pooled RII ranking of general construction labour productivity factors from 10 Middle East/Southeast-Asia surveys; it is not masonry-specific and not Indian. Only the Ouga et al. block-laying paper appears in the reference list (p. 194, 217) as background; none of the 10 pooled studies is a masonry study.
- Usable as supporting literature for candidate risk factors that also appear in masonry: supervision, skilled-labour shortage, change orders, drawing/specification clarity, material shortage, rework, incentive schemes, payment delay, weather, absenteeism, tools/equipment, storage, overtime, fatigue. The pooled RII values give an external benchmark for ordering, not a measure of masonry risk in India.
- Table 2 (p. 199) cites Attar, Gupta and Desai 2012 (India, 15 factors) and Thomas and Sudhakumar 2013 / Sangole and Ranit 2013 appear in text/references, but only as citations; their results are not analysed here beyond a list of top-5 factors for Attar et al.
- Plain use: cite as a regional meta-analytic ranking for factor identification; do not use RII numbers as probabilities or as Indian masonry rankings.

## 6 Limitations
- Scopus only, titles only, 2000-2020, journal articles; authors acknowledge the single-database limit (p. 215).
- Only 10 studies, one per country; selection by availability of RII; "Middle East" set includes Malaysia (Abdul Kadir) and Vietnam (Toan), which are not Middle East countries by the authors' own list (p. 194 vs Table 4, p. 203).
- Meta-analysis pools RII values from surveys with different respondents, scales and factor lists; heterogeneity is acknowledged only to justify a random-effects model; no Q, I-squared or tau values are printed (NR).
- Factors in at least 3 studies only; different studies report different subsets (many cells empty), so pooled values rest on 3 to 10 sources per factor.
- Table 7 omits two of the 35 factors (Delays in responding; Type of project) although the text ranks them; Table 3 total (21) differs from the 65 articles in text without explanation; the supervision paragraph is internally inconsistent about Qatar; Table 7 CI printed as upper-lower; CI half-width is identical (0.050) for every factor, which is not explained.
- Produced from published RII values; no primary data; context is mainly expatriate-labour Gulf construction.

## 7 NR / NC items
